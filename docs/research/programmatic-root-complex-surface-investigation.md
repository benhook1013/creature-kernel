# Programmatic root-complex surface investigation

Status: Candidate plan; Ben-authorized bounded investigation; non-normative

This is the settled plan for the next small investigation. It is an
experiment-local, pre-Readiness-4 activity using explicitly identified
authored/prepared input. It introduces no production `GeometryRequest` or
`GeometryResult` contract, production-core dependency, claimed resolved graph,
or durable domain, chart, or cage identity. It cannot satisfy or replace the
DR-0013 Stage 1 proof. DR-0009 and DR-0010 remain parked. Any promotion stops
for the appropriate decision record, canonical architecture work, and Ben's
approval.

## Named question and scope

The named human question is the directional root-complex continuation
checkpoint:

> Does a programmable surface-domain/subdivision-cage representation produce a
> credible ribcage-waist-pelvis assembly with readable neck, axilla, and
> hip/thigh-root boundaries, sufficient to justify extending the
> representation?

Passing is not Stage 1 or topology acceptance and does not authorize distal
anatomy. The candidate scope is the ribcage, waist, pelvis, neck port, bilateral
shoulder/axilla roots, and bilateral hip/thigh roots. Use the standard neutral
first. Only after credible main-thread visual inspection may unchanged rules
expand to the exact five ordered profiles:

- `standard_neutral_reference`
- `compact_broad_short_limb_large_head`
- `tall_narrow_long_legged`
- `slender_long_limb`
- `stocky_broad_chested`

Keep a separate standard-profile tail-present/tail-absent subcase. The core
outside that local port is unchanged, and the absent-tail case introduces no
absent tail identities. Compare front, side, and three-quarter final-skin
renders against the frozen failed baseline at the same framing and scale.

## Frozen anatomy hypothesis

The candidate tests non-elliptic anatomical sections and longitudinal
curvature; overlapping and directionally tapered ribcage-waist-pelvis masses;
thighs seated within the pelvis; and a neck emerging through the
thoracic/shoulder assembly. The relevant readable boundaries are shoulder cap,
axillary concavity, iliac/hip transition, and medial-thigh separation. These
are a frozen investigation hypothesis and visual criteria, not a product
anatomy contract.

## Private decomposition

Keep the implementation small and candidate-local. Its private decomposition
is:

1. prepared source projection;
2. surface intent;
3. domains, charts, junctions, and ports;
4. control/subdivision cage;
5. provisional causal binding;
6. evaluated mesh; and
7. a thin evidence renderer.

These are experiment-private layers, not accepted architecture, production
identity, or a durable chart/cage model.

The prepared projection may contain source-derived measurements, frames,
landmarks, section parameters, and provenance. It may not provide the finished
cage vertices, quad layout, or final shape. The shared surface program owns one
profile-independent topology grammar and derives cage-control coordinates from
those prepared controls through shared formulas. A topology template may be
hard-coded only as experiment-local grammar; profile IDs may select data and
provenance but never geometry code or stored final cage coordinates.

## Build order and complexity boundary

Before geometry coding, freeze the exact measurable trial contract and the
complexity cap. Then proceed in this order:

1. build a minimal neutral program and direct skin render;
2. perform the main-thread visual check;
3. apply the exact-five expansion only with the unchanged rules if that check
   is credible;
4. add only the causality and geometric validators that are needed;
5. run rejection-only static construction probes after static viability; and
6. add the thin gallery adapter last.

Any field is limited to clearance/collision diagnostics or a bounded
optimization energy over declared cage degrees of freedom. It may not alter
topology or remesh, directly move render-only vertices, add unbound patches,
or add profile patches. An inexpressible repair is a stop condition.

## Predeclared trial contract

Before geometry coding, the trial contract must state:

- continuity type and tolerance;
- tessellation and self-intersection exclusions;
- orientation and zero-area thresholds;
- deterministic environment and comparison procedure;
- profile-scaled corridors and keep-outs;
- allowed formulas and forbidden profile-ID branches;
- control perturbation, effect, and off-target bounds;
- finite code, dependency, special-case, and adapter caps; and
- the definition of one shared correction round.

The correspondence contract must additionally define oriented ordered
boundaries; transition and seam multiplicity; junction, ring, branch, pole,
and extraordinary-vertex rules; cross-profile control mapping and landmark
order; subdivision and render embedding; and ambiguous, invalid, and
multi-contributor states. Control IDs are revisioned artifact IDs only; they
are not durable domain or chart identity.

## Evaluated-surface gates

Gates operate on the evaluated surface, not only on the control cage. Check:

- source coverage and the causal dependency matrix;
- rejection of fixture-supplied final cage coordinates or profile-selected
  topology/geometry code;
- shared rules;
- topology and correspondence;
- continuity;
- self-intersection and degeneracy;
- neck, axilla, groin, and medial-thigh clearances; and
- silhouette evidence.

Diagnostic cut caps are excluded from anatomy judgments. A passing result
remains only a bounded reason to consider extending this representation; it is
not a Stage 1/topology result, distal-anatomy authorization, or production
geometry claim.

## Stop rules and exclusions

Stop after any of the following: a profile-bespoke geometry or topology fork;
ambiguous correspondence; global retopology for a module; negative-space
failure; a hidden second skin path; or three frozen shared correction rounds
without a credible root complex.

Explicitly excluded are full Stage 1, distal anatomy, rig/weights/shared
pose/deformation, simulation, packaging, Godot/engine work, and permanent
topology or backend selection. DR-0009/0010 remain parked throughout. A
promotion, production dependency, canonical domain/chart/cage identity, or
architecture change requires the appropriate canonical updates and Ben's
recorded approval.
