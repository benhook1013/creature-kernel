## Question

Can a single welded surface assembled from explicit, interacting anatomical
domains produce a credible standard-neutral ribcage-to-pelvis root complex
with a readable neck, shoulder/axilla junctions, and hip/thigh roots, without
any layer silently bypassing or rewriting another?

This is a representation-direction question. It is not a claim that the
result is a complete humanoid, an animation-ready body, a runtime skeleton,
or a production geometry backend.

## Hypothesis

Giving thorax, neck root, abdomen/lumbar bridge, pelvis, bilateral
shoulder/axilla roots, and bilateral hip roots explicit surface ownership,
local charts, and shared welded junctions will make the causal path from
authored anatomy to evaluated skin inspectable and will produce clearer root
anatomy than the failed single axial ring grammar.

The hypothesis fails if the candidate needs unowned geometry, duplicated or
hidden skin, render-only corrections, profile-specific geometry, a solver, or
more than the allowed shared correction rounds to make the neutral result
credible.

## Decision impact

A credible neutral result, after all technical gates, permits an unchanged
exact-five profile run and a later human checkpoint about whether this
representation is worth extending. It does not approve production use.

An uncured neutral failure rejects this candidate. It does not reject
programmatic or hybrid surfaces globally. A materially different
representation then requires a new recorded runway and direction; this
experiment does not silently fall back to another approach.

## Ephemeral experiment interfaces

The following are conceptual boundaries for this experiment only. They are not
serialized production schemas, canonical identifiers, or additions to
`spec/`:

- **Prepared intent**: admitted source addresses and roles, frames, landmarks,
  section measurements, module state, and provenance.
- **Domain plan**: one owner for each domain interior and its local chart,
  including declared boundary participation.
- **Junction plan**: the shared boundary between incident domains, its ordered
  interface curve, port relationship, and continuity obligations; the declared
  junction is the boundary's single construction owner and records all incident
  domains.
- **Causal binding**: source address/role to derived parameter, control,
  subdivision stencil, and evaluated-surface lineage.
- **Surface artifact**: one welded control/evaluated surface with ephemeral
  domain, junction, port, chart, contributor, and diagnostic metadata.

The construction layer owns base faces, domain interiors, and each declared
junction's shared boundary; each junction records all incident domains. The
semantic layer carries causality from authored addresses and roles through
derived parameters and subdivision stencils. The evaluated surface carries
contributor and chart lineage, which may name multiple domains. These layers
may reference one another through explicit bindings but may not silently
replace one another.

Every base face and control has exactly one construction owner: a domain
interior or a declared junction. Every declared junction is the single
construction owner of its shared boundary, which is represented once in the
welded surface and records all incident domains. Every evaluated surface
element has valid contributor/chart lineage. There is no unowned output. A
categorical colour or legend may expose lineage in a diagnostic, but it is
never evidence of causality by itself.

## Topology and junction requirements

The candidate is one connected, orientable, welded surface with the five
declared open ports. It must have no duplicate overlapping skin pieces,
unowned faces, seam-only contacts, accidental holes, or topology selected by a
profile. Every boundary and junction is explicit in the design record.

The junction network must make these relationships inspectable:

- a neck-root junction that exposes a neck stem rather than a mound in the
  thorax;
- shoulder/axilla junctions whose shoulders depart below the neck and leave a
  readable axillary hollow around each arm port;
- an abdomen/lumbar bridge between thorax and pelvis rather than one universal
  torso ring progression; and
- hip-root junctions where each pelvis wraps over and seats the thigh root,
  with the thighs descending from beneath the pelvis.

Shared boundaries use the same control curve, not two surfaces made to
coincide. The candidate must report positional continuity and the frozen
normal/fold measures. It must not claim mathematical C1 or G1 continuity at
extraordinary vertices or open ports without a separate proof.

## Causality and ownership requirements

Before rendering, a preflight must prove:

1. Every admitted geometry-driving source address, role, landmark, frame,
   section measure, module-state input, and shared scalar has a downstream
   causal consumer or is explicitly classified as non-geometric.
2. Every domain interior, junction, port, base face, and control has exactly
   one construction owner.
3. Every evaluated element carries contributor/chart lineage, including
   multi-domain junction contributions where applicable.
4. Each must-affect definition names a baseline, perturbation, in-target
   evaluated-vertex set, scale `S`, frozen minimum movement relative to `S`,
   off-target set, and frozen locality tolerance.
5. A fresh baseline-versus-perturbed build must move actual in-target evaluated
   vertex coordinates by at least that scale-relative minimum, and the final
   mesh bytes must differ by serializing those changed coordinates.
6. Off-target evaluated vertex coordinates remain within their frozen locality
   tolerance.
7. Metadata, provenance, dependency, manifest, or ownership-label movement
   without the required evaluated-coordinate and final-mesh movement fails.
8. Topology, IDs, formulas, and ownership remain unchanged under admitted
   perturbations.
9. The renderer consumes the evaluated surface and its lineage; it cannot
   add geometry, alter vertices, or use a second hidden skin.

Provenance is not proof of effect. A listed dependency with no geometric
effect fails the must-affect gate. Conversely, a contributor label without a
causal binding is not accepted as an explanation.

## Structural and geometric gates

The following gates apply to the actual welded evaluated surface and its base
construction, not to a synthetic diagnostic object:

- all values are finite and the surface is connected, orientable, and a valid
  welded quad surface with exactly the five declared ports;
- control/quadrilateral counts remain within the fixed caps and all topology,
  junction, port, and extraordinary rules match the frozen design;
- no face, edge, boundary, or control is unowned or duplicated;
- shared junctions satisfy the frozen positional continuity and normal/fold
  bounds;
- no degenerate edges/faces, invalid winding, accidental holes, or
  non-adjacent self-intersections exist at either evaluated level;
- the five ports preserve their frozen orientation and remain usable after
  subdivision;
- neck, both axillae, groin, and both medial-thigh clearances remain positive
  under the candidate-specific geometric measures;
- candidate-specific measures report neck exposure, shoulder descent,
  axillary concavity, pelvic wrap, and downward thigh-root orientation
  independently; and
- all must-affect/locality, forbidden-input, profile-neutrality, and
  deterministic-artifact checks pass.

Clearance and continuity thresholds are not invented in this plan. The frozen
design contract records them before coordinates and each is evaluated
independently; one aggregate score is forbidden. Human vision decides whether
positive geometry actually reads as anatomy.

## Correction stop

The initial implementation is followed by at most two shared correction
rounds. A round is one coherent, review-triggered change to already-frozen
shared construction formulas or tunables, applied consistently to both sides
and all relevant domains, followed by full regeneration and inspection.

A correction may not change the patch layout, port cardinality, topology,
ownership model, source admission, subdivision rule, gates, or scope. Such a
change is a new candidate and requires a new runway; it cannot be hidden as a
third correction. Render-only offsets, profile branches, duplicated skin,
solver behaviour, and threshold relaxation are immediate stop conditions.

If the neck, shoulder/axilla, and hip/thigh-root visual criteria are not
credible after the initial build plus two shared corrections, mark this
candidate rejected/inconclusive in its eventual evidence record, archive it,
and stop. Do not run exact-five, add a tail, build distal anatomy, or continue
parameter tuning.
